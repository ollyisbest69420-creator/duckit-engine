Refund Policy

Last updated {{YEAR}}

Pro costs {{PRO_PRICE}} and is billed through Stripe. This policy explains when we refund a payment and how to ask for one. It sits alongside your statutory rights, which it never reduces.

1. Free plan first

The free plan includes three full exports and every feature of the engine, apart from the length cap. Please try it before subscribing: the demo clips and a three-minute export are enough to judge whether the ducking suits your material.

2. Cancelling

  • Open the billing portal from the app and cancel at any time.
  • Cancellation stops the next renewal; Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • We do not pro-rate the remainder of a billing period, but no further charges are taken.
  • If a renewal was charged after you cancelled, contact us and we will refund it in full.

3. When we refund

  • Duplicate or accidental charges — always refunded in full.
  • First subscription within 14 days — refunded if you have not exported a full-length file with Pro.
  • Service unavailable — if the engine cannot process your files for a sustained period, we refund the affected period.
  • Technical failure on our side — refunded when we cannot produce a working export and cannot fix it for you.

4. When we cannot refund

  • Months already used to export full-length files.
  • Requests made more than 60 days after the charge, unless local law requires otherwise.
  • Dissatisfaction with a creative result that matches the settings you chose — the engine is a mixing aid, not a guarantee of taste.
Consumers in the UK and EU normally have 14 days to withdraw from a digital subscription. Because Pro unlocks content immediately, that right ends once you export a full-length file, but nothing here removes any other statutory right you may have.

5. How to request a refund

  1. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the address on the Stripe receipt.
  2. Include the date of the charge and the last four digits of the card, or the invoice number.
  3. Tell us briefly what went wrong so we can fix the underlying issue.

We reply within two business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method, usually within five to ten business days depending on your bank.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a chargeback: it is almost always faster. A chargeback freezes the subscription while the card network investigates.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy go to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. It is governed by the laws of {{JURISDICTION}}.
