{{COMPANY_NAME}} processes audio you upload so it can return a mixed export. This page explains what we store, why we store it, and how long it stays. It applies to the editor, the demo clips, and the billing flows on this domain.

2. No account, no audio archive

There is no sign-up. Your browser is recognised by a signed, random cookie that carries no personal details. Audio is processed to produce your download and is not used to train models, sold, or shared for advertising.

3. Legal bases

  • Contract — processing your uploads and managing your subscription.
  • Legitimate interests — security, abuse prevention, and aggregate product metrics.
  • Consent — optional analytics where local law requires it; you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legal obligation — keeping billing records for tax purposes.

4. Processors we rely on

  • Stripe — payment processing, subscriptions, and invoices.
  • PostHog — product analytics and feature flags, only when a project token is configured.
  • Our hosting provider — compute and storage for the engine.

Each processor is bound by a data-processing agreement. Where data leaves your region, transfers rely on standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

5. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, a restriction on processing, or portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Because there is no account, most requests are handled by quoting your identity cookie or a Stripe receipt. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will respond within one month.

You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority, including the regulator in {{JURISDICTION}}.

6. Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS, cookies are signed and scoped, secrets stay server-side, and outputs live in a private directory that is only reachable through your session. No system is perfect: use a unique password for your Stripe account and tell us about anything suspicious you notice here.

7. Children

The service is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly process their data.

Deleting your download early is easy: the link expires after 24 hours regardless, and clearing your cookies detaches future visits from the stored identity.

8. Changes and contact

Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect. Questions about this policy? Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
